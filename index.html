How we work

A few house rules.

We bend the rules inside our games. Outside them, we keep these.

  • Small and polished

    We'd rather ship one thing that feels great than ten that feel fine. Every screen, sound and animation gets fussed over.

  • Private by default

    No ads, no accounts, nothing that needs your name. This website doesn't even set a cookie. Our privacy policies say exactly what leaves your phone.

  • Tricky, but fair

    Our games cheat, openly and on purpose, but they never cheat you. Every trick can be caught if you're paying attention.

The studio

Who's behind the curtain?

TrollCoding is a small, independent software studio (and a fox with opinions). We do everything ourselves, from the first sketch to the store listing: design, code, art direction, testing and the occasional argument with the fox.

We build for Android first, in English and Spanish, and we like apps you can open, enjoy for five minutes and close with a grin.
